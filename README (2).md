# Topic 6 & Life Safety Reviewer

A mobile-friendly reviewer for two topics:

- **Topic 6 – On-Site Sewage Treatment (OSST):** septic tanks, bacteria, sludge and scum, capacity, construction, size and location
- **Life Safety Systems:** passive and active fire protection, fire suppression systems and components, fire water distribution and sprinkler types
- 161 questions, answered as Multiple choice, Identification, True or False, or Mixed
- **Flashcards:** flip each card, mark it "Got it" or "Study again", shuffle, hide known cards
- 9 topics, an Overall mode, and an Easy / Medium / Hard difficulty quiz; progress saves on your device

## Files

- `index.html` – the site (built from `app.html` by `build.sh`)
- `app.html` – the page source
- `questions.js` – the question bank
- `questions-em-backup.js` – the previous Engineering Management bank (not loaded by the page)
